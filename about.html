Born in the Current.

BlueWake started as a single kayak and a passion for silence in the central Florida mangroves. Today, we are the premier provider of luxury paddle expeditions for those who prioritize stealth over speed.

The Mission

To provide elite access to untouched marine environments without leaving a trace. Preservation is our primary payload.
